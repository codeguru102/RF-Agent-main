(1) The configured task score reflects the agent's actual task score or success rate after training under the current reward function.
(2) If the values for a certain reward component are near identical throughout training, then RL may not be able to optimize that component as it is written.
(3) If some reward components' magnitudes are significantly larger or smaller than the others, those components may not be conducive to policy learning.
(4) Use failures, constraint violations, instability, weak final scores, and the optional result feedback text to diagnose reward issues.
(5) Higher task score is better unless the task config says otherwise.
